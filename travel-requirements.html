Travel Requirements

Prepare for your journey with the documents and information you may need before travelling internationally.

International Travel Requirements

Before travelling to another country, passengers should make sure they have the necessary documents and meet the entry requirements of their destination.

Valid Passport

Make sure your passport is valid for your journey and has enough remaining validity for your destination.

Visa or Travel Authorization

Check whether your destination requires a visa, e-visa, electronic travel authorization, or visa on arrival.

Flight Ticket

Keep your confirmed flight booking and travel itinerary available when checking in and entering your destination country.

Accommodation Details

You may need proof of accommodation such as a hotel reservation or an invitation from your host.

Proof of Funds

Some countries may require proof that you have enough funds to support yourself during your stay.

Health Requirements

Check whether your destination requires vaccination certificates, health documents, or other medical requirements.

Travel Insurance

Travel insurance is recommended and may be required by some countries or for certain types of visas.

Supporting Documents

Depending on your destination and purpose of travel, you may need additional documents such as invitation letters, employment documents, or school admission letters.

Important Information

Travel and immigration requirements vary depending on the destination country, nationality, and purpose of travel. Passengers should confirm the latest requirements before starting their journey.
